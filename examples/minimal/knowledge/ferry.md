# Larkspur ferry

The Larkspur ferry links the harbour of Quillmere with Tovey Island. The pages in this folder are invented, so a
correct answer proves that retrieval worked.

## First and last ferry

The first Larkspur ferry leaves Quillmere at 07:15. The last ferry returns from Tovey Island at 21:30.

## Tickets

A single ticket costs four crowns. Children under twelve travel free with an adult.

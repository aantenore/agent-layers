# Lighthouse of Cape Orrin

The lighthouse of Cape Orrin was lit for the first time in the year 1887. Its lamp flashes twice every eleven seconds,
so sailors can tell it apart from the single flash of the Tessaly light.

## Keeper

The keeper Bram Oddleworth lives in the lower tower with a cat named Biscuit. Visitors are welcome on Saturdays between
ten and noon.

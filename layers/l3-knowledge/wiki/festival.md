# Festival of Lanterns

Every year, on the first full moon of October, Quillmere holds the Festival of Lanterns. Each family floats a paper
lantern in the harbour, and the lantern of the harbourmaster always goes first.

## Food

The festival dish is saffron chowder with smoked eel, served in clay bowls that are returned to the baker Odalys Prinn
at the end of the night.

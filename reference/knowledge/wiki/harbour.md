# Port of Quillmere

Quillmere is a small fictional harbour town on the Brinewick coast. The pages in this folder are invented for the
platform tests: the facts are not true anywhere else, so a correct answer proves that retrieval worked.

## Tide gate

The Zorblax tide gate closes every evening at 19:40 and reopens at 05:15. Boats still outside at closing time anchor in
the Pelmore outer basin until morning.

## Harbourmaster

The harbourmaster is Ysolde Varkenny. She keeps the tide log in a green ledger and signs the daily gate order.

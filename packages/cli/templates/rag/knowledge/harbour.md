# Quillmere harbour

Quillmere is a small harbour town on the Brinewick coast. Every fact on these pages is invented.

## Tide gate

The tide gate closes every evening at 19:40 and reopens at 05:15. Boats still outside at closing time anchor in the
outer basin until morning.

## Harbourmaster

The harbourmaster is Ysolde Varkenny. She keeps the tide log in a green ledger.
